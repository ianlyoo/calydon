How a hunt works

Commit before the deadline

Lock CAL in the current hunt. Weight is stake × steps left, so an early spear counts up to 4 times. The first commit takes First Spear.

Reach the HP: the boar falls

Each hunter claims the stake back plus a share of the hoard by weight. The next boar has 1.1× the HP.

Miss it: the boar escapes

Each hunter claims the stake minus 10%, and that part burns, so the supply falls. The next boar has 0.7× the HP.
